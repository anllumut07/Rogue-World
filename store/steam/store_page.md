## Detailed Description

### Overview

Rogue World is a grand strategy game inspired by Hearts of Iron IV, but set in the modern world (2026+). You don't just play as a country — you play as a **character** who rises through the ranks to lead a nation. Every decision matters, every system interconnects, and the simulation runs on deterministic logic — no hidden RNG.

### Key Features

**🎭 Character-Driven Gameplay**
- Create your leader: choose background (politician, military, academic, activist, business)
- Progress through a career: education, party politics, military ranks, or direct leadership
- 7 attributes (Diplomacy, Administration, Intelligence, Charisma, Military, Economy, Law) shape your effectiveness
- Age, health, family, and succession mechanics

**🌍 Modern World Simulation (50+ Detailed Countries)**
- Real-world data: population, GDP, military strength, nuclear capabilities, resources
- Major powers: USA, China, Russia, EU nations, India, Brazil, Turkey, and more
- Factions: NATO, EU, BRICS, SCO, African Union, ASEAN, Arab League, OPEC
- Dynamic relationships, world tension, and diplomatic crises

**⚙️ Deep Systems (All Interconnected)**
- **Economy:** 17 indicators (GDP, inflation, unemployment, corruption, Gini, education, health, etc.) with formula linkages
- **Technology:** 73 technologies across 9 categories (Era 1990-2020), research slots, variant designer
- **Military:** Division templates (17 battalion types), armies, province-based combat, frontlines, naval/air
- **Diplomacy:** Relations, factions, guarantees, world tension, peace conferences, sanctions, agreements
- **Politics:** 20 constitutions, 9 laws, parliament/parties, elections, authoritarian mechanics, cabinet ministers
- **Espionage:** 4 mission types, agent traits, counter-intelligence, agent rental pool
- **Space Program:** 10 missions from rocketry to Mars colonization
- **Nuclear:** Full program from enrichment to doctrine to use
- **Climate:** CO2/warming, 23 country profiles, 9 policies, 3 agreements, tipping points
- **Migration:** 4 border policies, crisis types, assimilation/return mechanics
- **Disasters:** 7 types with intervention choices and event chains
- **International Organizations:** 9 orgs with membership, voting, bonuses/penalties

**🎮 Modern UI/UX**
- 18 fully responsive panels (≤1152×648) with consistent theming
- HOI4-style decision panel, diplomacy tabs, statistics dashboards
- Search, tooltips, progress bars, chips, stat bars, empty states
- Mini-map, country cards, library/encyclopedia

**🔬 Deterministic & Testable**
- Custom xorshift64* RNG — no `new Random()` anywhere
- Save/Load with A==B verification (10-year soak test passes)
- 70+ automated headless tests (balance, nuclear, space, war, focus, etc.)
- Clean build: 0 errors / 0 warnings

### Gameplay Loop

1. **Choose Scenario:** Modern World (2026) or Historical 1936
2. **Create Character:** Background, attributes, appearance (procedural portrait)
3. **Select Country:** 50 detailed nations or 130+ generic
4. **Weekly Agenda:** 6 slots for meetings, rallies, press, summits, training
5. **Monthly Report:** Advisor summary, warnings, decisions
6. **React & Decide:** Events, crises, disasters, opportunities — every week brings choices
7. **Guide Your Nation:** Economy, military, diplomacy, tech, laws, elections
8. **Achieve Victory:** Economic, military, territorial dominance or survival
